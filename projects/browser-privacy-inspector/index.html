Why combinations matter

Browser fingerprinting

A screen size or timezone alone is usually not identifying. Combined with many other signals—such as graphics hardware, language, and browser capabilities—they can form a pattern that is more distinctive. Trackers may use that pattern to recognize a browser even without cookies.

This demo does not create or store a fingerprint. It shows individual values only so you can understand the ingredients.
